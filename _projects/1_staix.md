**Role:** Volunteer Web Developer · [Live site](https://statsupai.org/STAIX2026/index.html){:target="\_blank"}

[STAI-X 2026](https://statsupai.org/STAIX2026/index.html){:target="\_blank"} (Statistics and Trustworthy AI for Cross-Domain Acceleration) is an international conference held at Harvard University, organized by StatsUpAI, an American Statistical Association interest group, and supported by the National Science Foundation.

I volunteered to build the conference website from scratch. I designed and developed the first version and iterated through **v2, v3, and v4** based on feedback from the organizers. After the conference secured sponsorship, a larger team joined to expand the site into its final version.

## What I did

- Designed and built the initial website structure, layout, and visual style
- Iterated through four versions (v1–v4) based on organizer feedback
- Created pages for the conference program, speakers, registration, and call for papers
- Designed the sponsorship section to showcase partner and sponsor logos
- Designed the conference's digital flyer for the call for papers and posters
