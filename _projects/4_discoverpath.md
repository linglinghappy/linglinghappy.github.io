DiscoverPath is a paper search engine for biomedical research built on knowledge graphs (KGs). Researchers in interdisciplinary fields often use different terms to describe similar ideas, which makes keyword search difficult. Instead of a plain list of results, DiscoverPath visualizes the papers and terms related to a query as a knowledge graph, helping users build a step-by-step "path" toward the most relevant papers.

The system was developed by the [Data Lab](https://cs.rice.edu/~xh37/){:target="\_blank"} at Rice University and accepted as a demo paper at CIKM 2023.
